How do you get IT problems sorted?

We're USask students building a tool that answers IT questions from the knowledge base, so you don't have to open a ticket for something that's already documented. Before we build more of it, we want to know whether that's a real problem or one we invented.

WHAT WE'RE ASKING

How often you've contacted IT support, what you try first when something breaks, and whether your last ticket was something you could have solved yourself with the right instructions.

That last one is the whole question. If most IT tickets are things people could have handled but couldn't find the answer to, there's something worth building. If they're not, we'd rather know now.

YOUR ANSWERS ARE ANONYMOUS

The survey collects no name, no email and no NSID. We never ask for your password and never will.

The prize draw is a separate form so your email is never stored alongside your answers. Entering is optional and you can take the survey without it.
